Make hazards visible

I conduct several CQD activities each month. In our stationary and moving SCAN 6 exercises, everyone identified the objects, but scanning took longer than anticipated. A child image near a tire made the discussion especially meaningful. Houma has received periodic recognition on safety calls for consistent participation.

03 / CUSTOMER RECOVERY

Follow through across accounts

I partnered with a support agent to resolve inconsistent billing and pricing across a customer’s accounts, clarify tax differences, and align the pricing structure. I followed up on the resolution and recognized the agent to her supervisors.

04 / INVENTORY CONTROL

05 / ROUTE OPERATIONS

Match schedules to customer needs

I coached drivers to identify customers whose delivery frequency did not match consumption. Drivers escalated those accounts to me, and I updated the system, including moving some customers from every two weeks to every four weeks. We also explained the new service standards.

06 / ACCOUNT RECOVERY

I contact customers with past-due accounts, have collected previously written-off balances, and help customers reestablish service. My approach combines clear communication, follow-through, and a practical path back to service.
